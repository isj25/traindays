    Vallikunnu railway station code - VLI

    Use station code VLI when you search trains to or from Vallikunnu on IRCTC. Entering the code is faster and avoids picking the wrong station with a similar name.

    Passengers travelling via Vallikunnu should use VLI on IRCTC so the correct station is selected. This code also works for live train status, PNR checks, and enquiry SMS.

    How to book a train ticket for Vallikunnu

    1. Open the IRCTC website or app and start a train search.
    2. Type VLI in the From or To box instead of guessing the full station name.
    3. Choose your travel date. General quota opens 60 days in advance at 8:00 AM IST (journey date not counted).
    4. Use the IRCTC 60 days calculator first so you know the exact morning to book.
    5. If general tickets are gone, try Tatkal one day before the journey — AC at 10:00 AM and Sleeper/2S at 11:00 AM.
    IRCTC general booking for trains from Vallikunnu (VLI) opens 60 days before the journey date at 8:00 AM IST. Calculate the exact booking date before you sit down to book.

    Train ticket booking from Vallikunnu

    Indian Railways tickets can be booked online on IRCTC or at a railway reservation counter. For Vallikunnu (VLI), online booking is usually the fastest way to get a confirmed berth, especially on busy routes and holiday weekends.

    General quota Opens 60 days ahead at 8:00 AM IST. Journey date is not counted in the 60 days.
    AC Tatkal Opens 1 day before travel at 10:00 AM IST for 1A, 2A, 3A, CC and EC.
    Sleeper / 2S Tatkal Opens 1 day before travel at 11:00 AM IST for SL and 2S classes.

    Booking tips for Vallikunnu (VLI)

    • Complete IRCTC profile and Aadhaar authentication before opening day.
    • Keep From/To saved as VLI so search is instant at 8:00 AM.
    • If you miss general booking, check the Tatkal calculator for the next window.
    • After booking, verify the ticket on the PNR status page.
