    Train ticket booking from Bevinahalu

    Indian Railways tickets can be booked online on IRCTC or at a railway reservation counter. For Bevinahalu (BNL), online booking is usually the fastest way to get a confirmed berth, especially on busy routes and holiday weekends.

    General quota Opens 60 days ahead at 8:00 AM IST. Journey date is not counted in the 60 days.

    Sleeper / 2S Tatkal Opens 1 day before travel at 11:00 AM IST for SL and 2S classes.
