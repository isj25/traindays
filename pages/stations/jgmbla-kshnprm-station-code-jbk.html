    Jgmbla Kshnprm railway station code - JBK

    Tickets to Jgmbla Kshnprm are booked against railway station code JBK. IRCTC accepts either the station name or this short code during train search.

    Passengers travelling via Jgmbla Kshnprm should use JBK on IRCTC so the correct station is selected. This code also works for live train status, PNR checks, and enquiry SMS.

    How to book a train ticket for Jgmbla Kshnprm

    1. Open the IRCTC website or app and start a train search.
    2. Type JBK in the From or To box instead of guessing the full station name.
    3. Choose your travel date. General quota opens 60 days in advance at 8:00 AM IST (journey date not counted).
    4. Use the IRCTC 60 days calculator first so you know the exact morning to book.
    5. If general tickets are gone, try Tatkal one day before the journey — AC at 10:00 AM and Sleeper/2S at 11:00 AM.
    IRCTC general booking for trains from Jgmbla Kshnprm (JBK) opens 60 days before the journey date at 8:00 AM IST. Calculate the exact booking date before you sit down to book.

    Train ticket booking from Jgmbla Kshnprm

    Indian Railways tickets can be booked online on IRCTC or at a railway reservation counter. For Jgmbla Kshnprm (JBK), online booking is usually the fastest way to get a confirmed berth, especially on busy routes and holiday weekends.

    General quota Opens 60 days ahead at 8:00 AM IST. Journey date is not counted in the 60 days.
