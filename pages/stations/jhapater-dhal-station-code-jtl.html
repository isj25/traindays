    Train ticket booking from Jhapater Dhal

    Indian Railways tickets can be booked online on IRCTC or at a railway reservation counter. For Jhapater Dhal (JTL), online booking is usually the fastest way to get a confirmed berth, especially on busy routes and holiday weekends.

    General quota Opens 60 days ahead at 8:00 AM IST. Journey date is not counted in the 60 days.
