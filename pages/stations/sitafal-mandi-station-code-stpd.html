    How to book a train ticket for Sitafal Mandi

    1. Open the IRCTC website or app and start a train search.

    Train ticket booking from Sitafal Mandi

    Indian Railways tickets can be booked online on IRCTC or at a railway reservation counter. For Sitafal Mandi (STPD), online booking is usually the fastest way to get a confirmed berth, especially on busy routes and holiday weekends.

    General quota Opens 60 days ahead at 8:00 AM IST. Journey date is not counted in the 60 days.
    AC Tatkal Opens 1 day before travel at 10:00 AM IST for 1A, 2A, 3A, CC and EC.
    Sleeper / 2S Tatkal Opens 1 day before travel at 11:00 AM IST for SL and 2S classes.
