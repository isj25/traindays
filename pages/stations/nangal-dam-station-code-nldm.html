    1. Open the IRCTC website or app and start a train search.
    2. Type NLDM in the From or To box instead of guessing the full station name.
    3. Choose your travel date. General quota opens 60 days in advance at 8:00 AM IST (journey date not counted).

    Train ticket booking from Nangal Dam

    Indian Railways tickets can be booked online on IRCTC or at a railway reservation counter. For Nangal Dam (NLDM), online booking is usually the fastest way to get a confirmed berth, especially on busy routes and holiday weekends.

    General quota Opens 60 days ahead at 8:00 AM IST. Journey date is not counted in the 60 days.

    Sleeper / 2S Tatkal Opens 1 day before travel at 11:00 AM IST for SL and 2S classes.
