2027 Holiday Train Booking Dates

When to book trains for each 2027 holiday, including the day before and two days before, and which dates make a long weekend.

Booking opens at 8:00 AM, 60 days before you board.

The orange date is the day before tickets open. Use it to log in and keep passenger details and payment ready. Tickets open the next morning at 8:00 AM. That morning is the date in the “IRCTC opens” column.

Friday, 1 January 2027 is New Year’s Day. To board on Thursday, 31 December 2026, IRCTC opens on Sunday, 1 November 2026 at 8:00 AM. To board on Wednesday, 30 December 2026, IRCTC opens on Saturday, 31 October 2026 at 8:00 AM. Every holiday below has those same two columns.

Green rows are long weekends: a holiday already joined to Saturday and Sunday, or one leave day that joins them.

Lunar festivals marked tentative, including Ramadan, Ramzan Id, Bakrid, Muharram, and Milad un-Nabi, can shift by a day when the moon is sighted.

Long weekend opportunities

A Friday or Monday holiday already joins Saturday and Sunday. A Tuesday or Thursday holiday becomes a longer break when you take the single day between the holiday and the weekend. “If observed” means the weekday is a restricted holiday, so only some offices close.
